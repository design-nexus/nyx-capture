4 · Record mode: the bar's toggles become recording options
Same bar, recording optionsWhere Edit and Delay were: system sound, mic, pointer, webcam, a 3 s countdown, and the format (MP4, WebM, GIF). Lit means on. They're remembered, and Settings → Recording holds the same choices.
Choosing works as for a shotDrag an area, click a window, or click empty space for the whole screen. Recording starts at once (or after the countdown).
